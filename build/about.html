Mumbai In-House Studio

Designed, Cut & Crafted
Under One Roof.

GENZSTORE operates our own proprietary design studio and garment production line in Mumbai. Every piece is drafted, sampled, hand-cut, and precision-tailored in-house.

In-House Production Line

From Initial Sketch to Final Stitch

We reject third-party white-labeling and mass-market factory outsourcing. At GENZSTORE, our in-house patternmakers and master tailors construct every silhouette in small, numbered batches right in Mumbai.

This vertical in-house integration gives us obsessive control over seam durability, drape balance, and premium tactile finishes without intermediate markups.

"By owning our design studio and production floor in Mumbai, we create contemporary Gen-Z luxury garments that rival international runway houses at honest direct pricing."
— GENZSTORE Mumbai Design Collective
Zero-Waste Craftsmanship

Artisanal Natural Textiles

Our textiles are spun from 100% natural fibers: organic wool, certified mulberry silk, and hand-finished French linen. In our workshop, digital precision pattern-cutting reduces textile off-cuts to under 3%.

Every remaining organic remnant is upcycled into dustbags and structural canvas pocketing.
